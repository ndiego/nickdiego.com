For the past seven months, I've been building a Magic: The Gathering web app called Spellbinder. Hobby in the sense that I'm building something that I've always wanted to build but never had the time or expertise to accomplish until now, and one that, while explicitly designed to have a monetization path that supports it long term, generating revenue is not the goal. 

For those who don't know me, especially those in the Magic: The Gathering ecosystem, hi! My name is Nick. I've been playing Magic since middle school, when I was first introduced to cards from the Mercadian Masks in the lunchroom.  

The Public Beta is currently available. You can sign up, create decks, and start tracking your collection for free, but if you're interested in supporting this project and want advanced features, there are additional paid tiers as well. 

As we all know, the Internet is monetized through advertising, but I've taken the decision that Spellbinder will remain ad-free. Though it will include affiliate links to partners like TCG Player, there may be future sponsorship opportunities available in the app for industry partners, promotion of upcoming events, etc. But there will be no traditional digital advertising within the app. 

While the app has been thoroughly tested, please expect bugs and areas that still need polish. If you do find anything and wish to share your experience, there's a feedback button on every page, or you can reach out directly on social or contact me here through my personal site. Updates are being shipped to the app every single day, with major feature enhancements coming weekly or multiple times a week for the foreseeable future.
